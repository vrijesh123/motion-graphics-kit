# Review: Lykos Wx-1 v1 (2026-11-17)

## Creative calls
- One continuous Three.js world for every shot (white wolfx.io page, a dark flip for space, a white studio).
  The interception diagram is SVG, and the readable text is HTML on top, so it stays crisp.
- The missile lies horizontal in the hero shot (like the reference), then stands upright for the
  exploded view, because a tall stack fills a vertical frame.
- The reference's floor label is kept, but made two lines and turned to face the camera so it reads at 1080 wide.
- In shot 8 the "7 Payloads / Each with Its Own Engine" words sit above the nose rather than beside it:
  the vertical frame is too narrow for side labels. The shot 8 lead line is narrower (620 px) and leaves at 55.6 s
  so the stack never runs behind it.

## Measured vs looked at
- `npm run check`: 0 errors.
- Looked at about 60 snapshots across every shot and both sides of every cut.
- The render was checked by pulling frames from the MP4 itself: 70.02 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps, 2100 frames, an AAC
  track, rendered on the hardware GPU.
- Sound: generated by `tools/make_audio.py` from `cues.json`.
  - Measured on the render with `hyperframes audio`: -15.1 LUFS integrated, true peak -1.4 dB, no clipping, no silent
    gaps; the loudest moment is the lift-off at 15.0 s.
  - **Not listened to.**
- Motion between the sampled frames was not watched; play the preview or the MP4 for that.

## Warnings kept on purpose
- `composition_file_too_large`, `timeline_track_too_dense`, `nested_structure_needs_subcomposition`: the kit keeps a
  film in one `index.html` (same as the 2D WOLFx promo).
- `canvas_content_at_edge`: the contour floor runs to the frame edges by design.
- `contrast_aa_failure` at 66.1 s: measured mid-wipe on the end-card button's arrow; settles to white on black.

## Weakest spots, for the next pass
- Shot 5: the seven trails read as a bundle of arrows more than seven separate targets.
- Shot 4: the open nose close-up is heavy; the fairing halves are large dark shapes.
- The launch smoke and flame are simple sprites and cones; they read, but they are the least polished elements.
- Shot 7 opens on a mostly empty card for about 0.5 s before the first path appears.
- The contour ripple at ignition (14-17.5 s) is in the code but barely visible: the shot 3 camera sits near the floor
  looking up. A higher camera at ignition would show it.
- Fixed before delivery:
  - frames depended on the previous frame for the Solid Fuel tag's anchor (stale matrices); now they don't;
  - the floor label and the Solid Fuel tag sat partly below the 1330 px text band; both moved;
  - the hover shadow kept a later shot's opacity when scrubbing back.
