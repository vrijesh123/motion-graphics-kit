## Found but not verified
- **Headcount conflicts; Quantiphi publishes none.**
  - LinkedIn: 1,001-5,000 band, with about 4,491 people listing it as their employer [S24].
  - Tracxn: 2,670 [S25].
  - Zippia: 1,554 (its revenue figure of $750K is clearly wrong, so this source is not used).
  - 2019 release: "more than 1,000 employees" [S26].
  - Don't put a headcount on screen without the client's number.
- **About-page counters.** The page has "AI Experts", "Projects Globally", "Customers" and "AI Transformations" counters [S2]. They only fill in when scrolled in a browser. Their values could not be read and are not used.
- **Funding.** Tracxn lists $23.5M over two rounds (first on 2018-09-28) and names British International Investment as an investor [S25]. Only the $20M Multiples round is confirmed by Quantiphi [S26].
- **Revenue.** Not published. LinkedIn data puts it in a $100M-$1B band [S24]; aggregator estimates vary.
- **Description conflict.** Releases up to April 2026 say "AI-first digital engineering company" [S6, S7, S15]. Pages and releases from autumn 2026 say "AI-native services company/partner" [S2, S3, S17]. The newer wording is used in this pack; confirm.
- **Tagline status.** "Solving What Matters" is in all three logo files [S9] and the CTA reads "Ready to Solve What Matters?" [S1]. The logo's alt text on the live site is only "logo", so whether the tagline is still current is unconfirmed.
- **Pronunciation.** "kwon-TIF-ee" (IPA kwɔntˈɪfi) comes from user-submitted audio [S28]. Confirm by ear from a Quantiphi video, e.g. the CEO piece on the YouTube channel [S22].
- **Video style.** Inferred from titles and lengths only; no Quantiphi video was watched [S22].
- **Testimonials.** Only the Illinois Tech quote is shown in full. The others are cut off at "Read More" [S1, S21].
