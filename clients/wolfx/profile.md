## How they work
- **Five engagement models** [S1, S6]:
  - **Project basis:** fixed price, fixed scope and timeline.
  - **Monthly retainer:** an ongoing team for continuous work and support.
  - **Consultation:** advice on stack, architecture and process.
  - **White labeling:** WOLFx as the hidden tech partner for marketing and design agencies.
  - **Offshore Development Center:** a dedicated team of developers, designers and PMs working only on one
    client's projects, on WOLFx infrastructure.
- **Four-step process** [S3, S6]:
  - Discovery & Planning → Design, Development & Execution → Testing & Launch → Ongoing & Support.
  - They also name it "CPMCD": Communication, Planning, Modelling, Construction and Delivering.
  - Planning involves business analysts, UI/UX experts and strategy experts [S3].
- **Collaboration:** open communication, feedback loops, adaptability, transparency [S6].
- **Six core values** [S2]:
  - Communication ("Clear, Concise and Confident")
  - Accountability
  - Trust
  - Gratitude
  - Efficiency ("try to be lazy without compromising the quality of work… working smarter, not harder")
  - Lifelong Learning ("Even a small ant can teach you a big lesson. Be humble." The site has a word-order
    typo, "big a lesson", fixed here)

## Building in public: WOLFx Cerberus [S10]
A blog post by Ruchit Kharwa (12 Feb 2026) describes WOLFx's internal multi-agent AI system, "an autonomous
business development department".
- **Six agents:**
  - Hunter: finds prospects.
  - Researcher: qualifies them.
  - Outreach: sends personalised outreach.
  - Follow-up: runs follow-up sequences.
  - Conversation: handles replies.
  - Closer: negotiates.
- **Status:** the first four were being built and the last two were planned. Circuit breakers protect the
  company's reputation.
- **Stack:** Django, Celery, LangGraph, LangChain.
- **Its figures are targets, not results:** 10+ new clients a month, 4-7× conversion, up to 70% lower cost.
  Never present them as results.

The blog's other themes are offshore team governance, API-first microservices, AI-driven cloud migration,
design-led MVPs, fintech modernisation and smart contracts [S10].
